## DIAGRAM 1: BOD/Dissolved Oxygen Curve (Ch 19)
[Source: ch19_2.pdf, slide 16]
**What to show**: X-axis = downstream distance; Y-axis = concentration (DO and BOD). 
Tw o curves — BOD (highest at pollution zone, decreasing through recovery zone) and DO (lowest at active decomposition zone, recovering later).
Three zones labeled: Pollution Zone | Active Decomposition Zone | Recovery Zone.
Horizontal line at 5 mg/L = EPA threshold.
**Exam risk**: High — classic diagram question

## DIAGRAM 2: Eutrophication Process (Ch 19)
[Source: ch19_2.pdf, slide 22]
**What to show**: Flow chart: Nutrient input (N,P) → Algal bloom → Dense mat forms → Bottom algae die → Bacterial decomposition → O₂ consumed → Fish kill. Lake cross-section showing stratified algae layer.
**Exam risk**: High — ecosystem effect concept

## DIAGRAM 3: Wastewater Treatment Plant (Ch 19)
[Source: ch19_2.pdf, slide 52]
**What to show**: Flow diagram: Raw sewage → Screens → Grit chamber → Primary sedimentation → Aeration tank (with air input + activated sludge recycle) → Final sedimentation → Chlorination → Discharge. A naerobic digester on side producing methane.
**Exam risk**: Medium — process description

## DIAGRAM 4: Atmosphere Structure (Ch 20)
[Source: ch20-part 2.1.pptx, slide 6]
**What to show**: Vertical profile from ground to 110 km. Four layers: Troposphere (0–12 km, temp decreases to -60°C), Tropopause, Stratosphere (12–50 km, temp increases, ozone layer at 25–30 km), Stratopause, Mesosphere, Mesopause, Thermosphere.
**Exam risk**: High — four layers in order (classwork Q15)

## DIAGRAM 5: Hadley Cells (Ch 20)
[Source: ch20-part 2.1.pptx, slide 10]
**What to show**: Cross-section of Earth from pole to pole. Three cells per hemisphere. Equatorial low pressure (rising warm air, rain), 30° high pressure (sinking dry air, deserts), 50–60° low pressure (rising, precipitation), polar high (sinking). Arrows showing circulation.
**Exam risk**: High — atmospheric circulation

## DIAGRAM 6: Greenhouse Effect (Ch 20)
[Source: ch20-part 3.pptm, slide 4]
**What to show**: Incoming solar radiation (visible/near-IR) → some reflected by atmosphere, some absorbed, some reaches surface → Earth warms → emits far-IR → greenhouse gases (H₂O, CO₂, CH₄) absorb and re-emit → some radiated to space, some back to surface.
**Exam risk**: High — core concept

## DIAGRAM 7: Ocean Conveyor Belt (Ch 20)
[Source: ch20-part 3.pptm, slide 23]
**What to show**: Global map with warm surface current (red) moving north in Atlantic → sinks near Greenland → cold deep current (blue) flowing south → east → north in Pacific → upwelling in North Pacific.
**Exam risk**: High — subjective question (classwork Q14)
