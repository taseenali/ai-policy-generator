## 4. PROCESSES & CAUSE-EFFECT CHAINS

### 4.1 Greenhouse effect mechanism
[Concept ID: ch20-029]
1. Sun emits visible/near-IR radiation
2. ~30% reflected by atmosphere; ~25% absorbed; ~45% reaches surface
3. Earth's surface absorbs and warms; re-emits as far-IR
4. Greenhouse gases (H₂O, CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, CFCs) absorb far-IR
5. Gases re-emit IR in all directions, including back to surface
6. This traps heat; keeps Earth ~33°C warmer than otherwise
- Must memorize: yes (process steps)
- Source: [ch20-part 3.pptm, slides 2–4; ch20 handouts.pdf, slides 52–55]
- Diagram: VISION_GAP — Fig 20.17 idealized greenhouse effect diagram

### 4.2 Ocean conveyor belt process
[Concept ID: ch20-030]
1. Warm surface water moves northward in Atlantic (Gulf Stream)
2. Arrives near Greenland at 12°–13°C
3. Cools to 2°–4°C in North Atlantic; becomes saltier/denser
4. Dense water sinks to bottom
5. Cold deep current flows south → east → north in Pacific
6. Upwelling in North Pacific starts warm shallow current again
7. Flow: ~20 million m³/sec (~100 Amazon Rivers)
8. Effect: Keeps N. Europe 5°–10°C warmer than otherwise
- Must memorize: yes (exam subjective)
- Source: [ch20-part 3.pptm, slide 22]

### 4.3 Positive feedback loop: warming → permafrost melt
[Concept ID: ch20-031]
Warming → permafrost melt → release of stored methane (CH₄) → more greenhouse warming → more permafrost melt
- Must memorize: yes
- Source: [ch20-part 3.pptm, slide 19]

### 4.4 Positive feedback: ice-albedo
[Concept ID: ch20-032]
Warming → snow/ice melt → darker surface exposed (vegetation/soil) → lower albedo → more solar absorption → more warming

## 7. LAWS, POLICIES, TREATIES

- **Paris Accords (2015)**: 132+ countries; binding agreement to combat climate change; limit warming to 2°C or less. [source: ch20-Lecture questions.pdf, p.4]
- **UN Framework Convention on Climate Change (1992 Earth Summit)**: 192+ nations; goal to stabilize GHG concentrations. [source: ch20-Lecture questions.pdf, p.4]
- **Clean Air Act**: 2007 Supreme Court ruled GHGs covered as pollutants; 2014 EPA began regulating coal-fired power plant emissions. Trump administration reversed some policies. [source: ch20-Lecture questions.pdf, p.4]
